Intro

Hi, I am Swetha Sri Jaladanki a Senior Full Stack Java Developer with over 7 years of experience designing and delivering scalable, secure, and high-performance enterprise applications across financial services, retail, and supply chain domains. I hold a Master’s degree in Computer Science from Purdue University and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Anna University, providing me with a strong academic foundation combined with extensive industry experience.

Currently, I work at Federated Hermes, where I contribute to a large-scale Financial Operations Platform that processes high-volume trade data, pricing updates, compliance rules, and portfolio validations. I specialize in building Spring Boot microservices, developing secure REST APIs using Spring Security and JWT, and optimizing SQL Server performance through query tuning and Hibernate optimizations. My work ensures high system reliability, data accuracy, and seamless integration with downstream reporting systems.

Previously, at Medline Industries, I played a key role in developing backend services for inventory and supply chain management systems supporting warehouse operations and real-time stock updates. I designed microservices using Spring Boot and Spring Cloud, implemented Kafka for real-time data synchronization across warehouses, and improved application performance by optimizing PostgreSQL queries. I also actively supported CI/CD pipelines and maintained high code quality through unit and integration testing.

Earlier in my career, I worked with retail platforms at HomeGoods and contributed to product catalog and pricing management systems, where I implemented Redis caching and Oracle database optimizations to reduce latency during peak loads. I began my professional journey at Tvisha Technologies, gaining strong hands-on experience in Java, Spring MVC, REST APIs, and database integration while working closely with clients and supporting production systems.

Across my career, I have worked extensively with Java, Spring Boot, microservices architecture, SQL and NoSQL databases, Kafka, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms including Azure, AWS, and GCP. I thrive in Agile environments, enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams, and take pride in delivering clean, maintainable, and test-driven code.

I am passionate about building robust backend systems, continuously learning new technologies, and contributing to solutions that drive real business impact. I am always eager to take on new challenges and create scalable, reliable software that supports organizational growth.
